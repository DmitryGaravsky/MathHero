# Child guide

Status — active instructions for MathHero 1.1. Screen names are described in
English here. In the app, labels follow the selected Spanish or Russian language.

1. Open MathHero. Child mode opens with the main practice button. Tap it to
   reach the ready or saved-practice screen, then tap Start or Resume.
2. Read the equation and enter the answer with the on-screen keypad. Use the
   erase key to fix a digit and the checkmark to submit. You can also type and
   press Enter on a physical keyboard or turn on the device keyboard.
3. A correct answer gets a short confirmation before the next question. After
   a mistake, the app shows the correct product and waits. You can copy the
   product for practice or select Next. The optional correction earns no
   extra attempt or stars.
4. To take a break, tap Pause. Resume the same exercise when ready. A regular
   practice contains 24 questions. Use End session to finish early or
   Save and exit to keep it available for later.

The result shows answers and progress recorded during the session and offers a
next action. Achievements are available from home. Switching away from the app,
opening the parent prompt or changing modes pauses an active exercise. Returning
to a saved question requires Resume. Feedback restored after leaving the active
practice waits for your next action.
