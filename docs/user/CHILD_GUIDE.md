# Child guide

In the app, names follow the selected Spanish or Russian language. Child mode
opens with a choice of multiplication tables or column arithmetic.

## Multiplication tables

1. Tap the counters card, then tap Start or Resume.
2. Enter the product with the on-screen keypad and tap the checkmark. You can
   also type and press Enter on a physical keyboard, or turn on the device
   keyboard.
3. A correct answer gets a short confirmation before the next question. After
   a mistake, the app shows the correct product and waits. Copy the answer
   for practice or select Next.
4. A regular practice contains 24 questions. Pause to take a break, end the
   session to see your result, or save and exit to return later.

The multiplication progress and achievements on Home describe your work
with the tables.

## Column arithmetic

1. Tap the notebook card, then choose Addition or Subtraction. Each operation
   has its own saved exercise.
2. Start at the units. After each digit, selection moves one place left
   until the last cell. Tap any answer cell to choose it, replace its digit,
   or erase it. Leave unused leading cells empty. Enter a zero if the answer
   is zero.
3. To record a carry in addition, tap the upper cell in the receiving column.
   The empty thousands cell can hold a carry. To record a borrow in
   subtraction, tap the top digit of the lending column, including a zero.
   Tap again to remove a mark.
4. Tap the checkmark to check your digits. A correct answer moves on after a
   short confirmation. After a mistake, the app highlights the first place
   to revisit. Choose Correct to edit your work or Ask for help to see the
   steps. You can use the same optional help before checking.
5. Tap Pause or Save and exit to keep your digits, marks and selected place.
   Pause also offers Skip exercise and End session. A complete practice
   contains six examples.

A physical keyboard can enter digits, select neighboring places with the
arrow keys, erase with Backspace or Delete, and check with Enter from the
answer cells. Opening the parent prompt or leaving the app pauses practice.
A resumed explanation starts closed. If you return to saved correct feedback,
select Next to continue.
