# Audience and positioning

Product reference for MathHero 1.2.

MathHero is for children practicing the 1–12 multiplication table or building
fluency with three-digit addition and subtraction after learning regrouping.
The child sees one exercise at a time, gets immediate feedback and can finish
a session early.

In [multiplication](../pedagogy/LEARNING_MODEL.md), difficult facts reappear
with other questions in between, factor order varies and mastered facts are
revisited. The adult can compare accuracy, fluency and recall after a delay.
The fox and stars acknowledge practice.

In [column arithmetic](../pedagogy/COLUMN_ARITHMETIC.md), fresh examples
revisit carry and borrow patterns. The child can make working notes and open
help on request. The adult can distinguish an independent first check from a
solution after correction. The timer runs in the background in both modules.
