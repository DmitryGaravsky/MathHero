# Audience and positioning

Status — active product reference for MathHero 1.1.

MathHero is for children learning the 1–12 multiplication table and adults
guiding that practice. The child sees one exercise at a time, gets immediate
feedback and can finish a short session early. The adult can see the difference
between facts the child answers correctly, facts they recall quickly, and facts
they have successfully recalled after a delay.

The trainer adapts from answer history. It reintroduces difficult facts with
other questions in between, varies factor order and periodically revisits
mastered facts. A timer measures active answering time in the background.
A correct answer gets the same confirmation at any speed. The fox and stars
acknowledge practice while the [learning model](../pedagogy/LEARNING_MODEL.md)
determines which facts need attention.
