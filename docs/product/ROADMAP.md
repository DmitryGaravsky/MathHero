# Scope and future ideas

Scope statement for MathHero 1.2.

MathHero 1.2 covers multiplication for factors 1–12 and addition and subtraction
with two three-digit operands. See [implemented features](FEATURES.md) and the
[1.2 delivery record](../DELIVERY-COLUMN-ARITHMETIC.md) for release verification
and publication status. The [1.2 device checklist](../DEVICE-ACCEPTANCE-1.2.md)
tracks the pending physical checks for this release. The completed
[1.1 device record](../DEVICE-ACCEPTANCE.md) covers the earlier multiplication release.

The [original MVP proposal](../specification.txt) lists inverse division facts,
column multiplication, long division and mixed operations as future ideas.
They have no scheduled release. Accounts, child profiles, backend services,
cloud sync, telemetry, voice input and competitive play are outside 1.2 scope.
